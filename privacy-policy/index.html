Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PT Juke Solusi Teknologi ("Juke", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Jukkidleware (the "Service").

Jukkidleware is a social media integration middleware that connects business applications, including Odoo, with supported social media platforms through their official APIs.

Jukkidleware may connect to authorized social media accounts through the respective platform's official authorization and API mechanisms.

By using the Service or connecting a social media account to Jukkidleware, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect or process:

  • Business or organization name.
  • Contact information, such as email address or phone number.
  • Social media account information when you authorize access to an account.
  • Information entered or submitted through connected business applications and workflows.

We only collect or process information that is necessary to provide the requested Service and integration functionality.

2.2 Social Media Platform Data

When you connect a social media account to Jukkidleware, we may access information made available through the applicable platform's official APIs and the permissions you authorize.

Depending on the platform and permissions granted, this may include:

  • Basic Profile Information: Information such as username, display name, profile picture, and platform-specific account identifiers.
  • Content Information: Information associated with social media content that is processed through the integration, such as content identifiers, URLs, captions, or other relevant metadata.
  • Engagement Information: Information related to supported social media activities and interactions when such functionality is enabled and authorized.
  • Authorization Information: Information required to maintain the connection between your authorized social media account and Jukkidleware.

The specific information accessed depends on the social media platform, the functionality being used, and the permissions you authorize.

Jukkidleware only accesses information that you explicitly authorize through the respective social media platform.

You may revoke access to Jukkidleware through the applicable account or authorization settings provided by the respective platform.

2.3 System and Integration Data

We may collect technical information necessary to operate and maintain the Service, including:

  • System logs.
  • Integration configuration information.
  • API usage information.
  • Technical information necessary to maintain the connection between Jukkidleware, connected business applications, and supported social media platforms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate Jukkidleware's integration functionality.
  • Connect authorized social media accounts with supported business applications, including Odoo.
  • Facilitate authorized communication between connected business applications and social media platforms.
  • Display or process social media information within connected business workflows where supported.
  • Support social media content and integration workflows authorized by the user.
  • Maintain and improve the performance, reliability, and security of the Service.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues and maintain integrations.
  • Provide customer and technical support.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and platform requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use information obtained through social media platform APIs for advertising, resale, or unauthorized profiling.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through Jukkidleware.

Information may be stored and processed within connected business applications, Jukkidleware infrastructure, and/or the hosting environment used to operate the Service, depending on the integration configuration.

We implement appropriate safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain the integration, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our applicable retention practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose or transfer information only when necessary to provide the Service, operate our infrastructure, comply with applicable law, or perform functionality that you have authorized.

Information may be shared with:

  • Social media platforms, when necessary to provide functionality authorized by you through their official APIs.
  • Business applications, including Odoo, when necessary to provide the requested integration functionality.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that support the operation, security, and maintenance of Jukkidleware.
  • Service providers that assist us in operating and maintaining the Service.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, when disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that third parties receiving information process it only for legitimate purposes related to the Service or as otherwise required by law.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing information we hold about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of your information.
  • Requesting restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Revoking authorization granted to Jukkidleware through a connected social media platform.
  • Requesting information about how your data is processed.

To exercise applicable rights or submit a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided in Section 10.

Please note that revoking access to a connected social media account may affect the availability of certain Jukkidleware features.

7. Third-Party Services

Jukkidleware integrates with third-party services and platforms, which may include social media platforms such as:

  • TikTok
  • Instagram
  • Facebook
  • Other supported platforms

Jukkidleware may also integrate with business applications such as Odoo.

Each third-party service operates under its own terms of service, privacy policy, and data practices.

Jukkidleware does not control the privacy practices, policies, availability, or functionality of third-party services.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies and terms of service of any third-party platform you connect to Jukkidleware.

8. Children's Privacy

Jukkidleware is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Service, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to:

  • Jukkidleware and its functionality.
  • Our data processing practices.
  • Applicable laws or regulations.
  • Third-party platform requirements.
  • Security and operational requirements.

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we handle information.
