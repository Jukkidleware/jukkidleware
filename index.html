What is Jukkidleware?

One Integration Layer for Your Business

Jukkidleware acts as a middleware layer between business applications and external social media platforms.

Instead of building and maintaining separate integrations between every system, Jukkidleware provides a centralized layer to manage communication between business applications and supported social media platforms.
