What is Jukkidleware?

One Integration Layer for Your Business

Jukkidleware acts as a middleware layer between business applications and external social media platforms.

Instead of building and maintaining separate integrations between every system, Jukkidleware provides a centralized layer to manage communication between business applications and supported social media platforms.

How It Works

From Business Workflow to Social Platform

01 — Connect

Users connect their social media account through the platform's authorization flow.

02 — Authorize

The user grants the permissions required by Jukkidleware.

03 — Integrate

Jukkidleware securely handles communication between the business application and the social media platform.

04 — Manage

Authorized social media activities can be managed through the connected business workflow.

Security & Privacy

Your Data, Your Authorization

Jukkidleware only accesses information and capabilities that users explicitly authorize through the respective social media platform.

Platform data is processed to provide the requested integration functionality and is not sold or used for unauthorized advertising or profiling.
