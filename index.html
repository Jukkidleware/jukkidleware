What is Jukkidleware?

One Integration Layer for Your Business

Jukkidleware acts as a middleware layer between business applications and external social media platforms.

Instead of building and maintaining separate integrations between every system, Jukkidleware provides a centralized layer to manage communication between business applications and supported social media platforms.

How It Works

From Business Workflow to Social Platform

01 — Connect

Users connect their social media account through the platform's authorization flow.

02 — Authorize

The user grants the permissions required by Jukkidleware.

03 — Integrate

Jukkidleware securely handles communication between the business application and the social media platform.

04 — Manage

Authorized social media activities can be managed through the connected business workflow.

Privacy Policy

PT Juke Solusi Teknologi — Social Media Middleware

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our Social Media Middleware connected to Odoo ("Service").

This Service uses social media platform APIs and is connected to our registered developer applications.

By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Business name
  • Contact details (such as email or phone number)
  • Social media account information when you authorize access
  • Information entered into Odoo related to customers, campaigns, or interactions

2.2 Platform Data (Including TikTok)

Through social media platform APIs (such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), we may process:

  • Basic Profile Information: Username, display name, avatar/profile picture, and unique account identifiers (e.g., TikTok Open ID).
  • Content Data: Metadata of your videos, carousels, and posts (such as IDs, URLs, and captions).
  • Engagement & Messaging Data: Comments, interactions, and direct messages sent to your social media business accounts, to enable real-time chat within Jukkidleware.

We only access data that you explicitly authorize via the respective platform. For TikTok users, you can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings or by disconnecting the account within the Jukkidleware dashboard.

2.3 System & Odoo Data

  • Logs
  • Integration configuration details
  • API usage data

We use the collected data to:

  • Ensure compliance with each platform's policies

We do not use platform data for advertising, resale, or unauthorized profiling.
